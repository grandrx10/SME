# Notes per card

Grep for the card here before changing it. `X` in "`.X.cs`" = the partial of `SpellServer` (rules), `WorldNet` (ops), `SpellPlans` (sizes).

## Arcane shield (everyone's, right click)
- `SpellServer.Shield.cs` (what it stops), `Combatant.Shield.cs` (health), `PlayerController.Shield.cs`, `AbilityCaster.Shield.cs` (key), `ShieldVfx.cs`, `GameUI.Shield.cs`. Not a card. Casting drops it; broken, it's back at 5 HP. The user's choices: auras, gazes, hazards, falls, judgment beams, a tornado's lift (it soaks the tornado's damage), hexes, The Chariot and burns already going ignore it; Death's charge is stopped but takes the whole shield; it can't be held inside someone else's Empress circle (`InEnemyEmpress`). Blocks wider than it's drawn on purpose. Icon: `CardArt/ArcaneShield`. Defense / attack dummies (U): `TestDummy.Kinds.cs`.

## Weapon
- **Sword** (Severance, Flowing Cut; was "Blade", the code still says so): `BladeVfx.cs`, `SpellServer.Blade.cs`. The user's `Resources/VFX/Sword.prefab` (grip = "Handle" child, blade along +Y). Fades in and out dithered (`SME/ToonFade`, the user asked for it). Hits flash the Chromatist's lens-shaped slash frames (`SlashFx`).
- **Hammer**: `HammerVfx.cs`, `SpellServer.Weapon.cs`. The user's `Resources/VFX/Hammer.prefab` (hands on "HandleR" / "HandleL", right one nearer the head; head at the origin, handle along +Y, striking face +X). `HammerMotion` drives arms and hammer. `SpellPlans.CraterRadius` drives the slam's bowl, who it hits and the ring.
- **Guns** (Rachmaninoff, Sebastian): `.Guns.cs`, `GunVfx.cs`. The user's Gunbound prefabs `Resources/VFX/Rachmaninoff.prefab`, `Sebastian.prefab` (barrel +X, held at 0.4 scale; TriggerPoint = right hand, OffhandHold = left hand, BarrelPoint = muzzle; recolour by swapping part materials, e.g. `Assets/SME/GunBlock.mat`). `GunMotion` drives arms and gun.
- **Sebastian**: paid when the charge begins; let go early = refund, interrupted = lost; 0.3 s wind-up before it fires; aims up and down too; body and gun stop turning when it's let go (`FaceLock(…, true)` holds the pitch; the camera stays free); only its caster sees the line. `SebastianRay` / `SebastianReach` give the line, the shot and who it hits.

- **Blade / Gun / Hammer elements** (were one Weapon element, now retired: no card uses it; Grapple Hook is a Gun). The four newer ones: `SpellPlans.Arms.cs`, `SpellServer.Arms.cs`, `WorldNet.Arms.cs` (ops 151-159), `ArmsVfx.cs`, `WolfgangVfx.cs`.
- **Hammer Spin**: held (Motor.Kneeling while winding up); owner `Blink(.., steady: true)` the whole way, so walls don't stop it; the server smashes cells `SpinLead` ahead along the same level path. `Spinning` counts as `Unmovable` (no knocks, still damage). The body spin is `SpellPlans.SpinAngle`: the animator turns the model by it, `HammerView` adds it to the yaw.
- **Wolfgang**: `Resources/VFX/Gun.prefab` (copied from Gunbound `Models/Gun.prefab`); its `Slide` parts sit on a `Slide` child of the template (`GunModel.Slide`). `WolfMotion` is where the pistols are held, shared by arms, view and server (bullets leave its muzzles). Body face-locked (yaw and pitch) for the whole card. Bullet holes: `BulletHole` raycasts a moment after the ground breaks.
- **Heavenly Slash**: the random roll is the cut's angle around the aim (`HeavenRotation` = `WaveRotation`), sent at cast (`HeavenRoll` op) so the caster's indicator shows it while hovering. Hover = `Blink` to where you already are. Flurry with `HeavenStrikes`.
- **Parry**: everything that lands on someone asks `ParryCatch(victim, foe, point)` first: `Hit` with damage, `Burn` (the first touch of fire; burn ticks never ask), and the holds (Undying Grasp, Stasis and Mind Hex taking hold, the Empress's chains, The Chariot's pin, a tornado, the Fly Trap, Attraction). A new immobilizer must ask it too. The dash is server-decided, sent to the owner (`ServerParryDash`); `ParryDashing` is `Invulnerable`. Pending flurries on the parrier are cancelled (`left = 0`).

## Necromancy (sickly green; `SpellServer.Necro.cs`)
- **Undead** are on their master's side (`Combatant.Team`, `Combatant.Allies`: no damage or knockback between allies, one side for winning the round). Summon Horde zombies and raised dummies are `TestDummy`s with a simple server AI.
- **Teams**: `Combatant.Team` follows a raised master up to their own master; `RaisedAgainst` = an Undead and its master's Team Deathmatch teammates (no damage). An Undead bitten to death just dies.
- **Undying** (passive, -10 max health): a dead player with it pending still counts as standing (`SpellServer.UndyingPending`).
- **Undying Grasp**: `.Guns.cs`, `GraspVfx.cs`. Held-in-place silence is `Combatant.Grasped`. The arms are cut out of the character's own mesh (`GraspArmModel`).
- **Necromatic Blast**: `LandNecro` in `.Hex.cs`, `NecroBallVfx.cs`. Its ball is a fireball with `necro` set (`FireballState` / `FireballFx`), so everything that handles fireballs in flight handles it; anything about fire must skip `necro`. Green ground: `VoxelWorld.Blight` (palette entries, like soot).
- **Skinwalker**: `PlayerController.Skin` (`SkinState`) is the skin worn: colour, face, name tag and moves. What a player can cast is `PlayerController.Hand`, never `Equipped`. The corpse is used up (`Combatant.ConsumeCorpse`). Off at the next round, when the loadout changes in practice, or when its key is held 1 s away from any corpse (`ShedSkin`). Kill feed and scoreboard keep the real name.

## Hex (purple; `.Hex.cs`, `HexVfx.cs`)
- Marks (the bars): `Combatant.Hexes` (`HexMarks`, up to 4 on a body, same kind twice is fine except Mind). Filled in `HexDamaged` (Shatter, Stasis) and `TickMarks` (Mind).
- **Shatter**: first `BodyStatus.ShatterUntil` (lifted, held in the air, nothing moves them: `GlassCase` in `ShatterVfx.cs`), then it goes off (`Shatter`, `GlassBurst`). A kill: `BodyShatter` (pieces aren't skinned; the biggest is named "Bone").
- **Stasis**: `BodyStatus.StasisUntil`. The rest of the attack that set it off (0.25 s) neither moves nor thaws them; burning never thaws. Anything that moves a body without a knock (a chariot's pin, the Empress's chains) calls `FreeToMove` first. The pose holds because `ProceduralAnimator` stops and `LiveRagdoll` goes kinematic.
- **Mind**: `BodyStatus.MindUntil/MindBy`. The victim's own machine (or the server, for a dummy) copies the caster's replicated velocity (world direction, whichever way either faces), turning, crouch and jumps (`SpellPlans.PuppetWish`).

## Flame
- **Flame bombs** (Remote Flame Bomb, Remote Detonation, Flame Bomb Toss): `.Bombs.cs`, `BombVfx.cs`. Stuck to the world, or to a body part (`SpellPlans.BombBone` finds it on the body, its ragdoll or its corpse). New lasting flames go in `BombInFlames`, new projectiles go in `HitBombs`, new flame explosions call `FlameSetsOff`, new water counts in `InWater`.

## Water (`WaterVfx.cs`, `SpellServer.Water.cs`)
- Every Water spell puts out fire it touches (fireballs, hellfire walls, burning ground, burning people); fire never breaks water.

## Ground / ice
- **Rock Swarm, Ice Spike**: `.Ground.cs`. Swarm rocks are voxel balls of radius `SwarmRockRadius` (drawn, hit and hit-by at that size), no colliders, placed by `WorldNet.SwarmPosition` on every peer. Ice is its own palette entries (`VoxelWorld.IceValue`, added only while applying ops so palettes match); fire near a spike crumbles it.

## Tarot (`TarotVfx.cs`, `TarotVfx2.cs`, `.Tarot.cs`, `.Tarot2.cs`)
- **The Chariot** rolls the caster into a ball (the user asked for it). `ChariotRadius` drives the ball, the controller, the hitbox (`Combatant.SetBalled`), the tunnel and who it runs over.
- **IX–XVI, Flame Thrower, Icy Breath, Grapple Hook**: `.Arcana.cs`, `.Breath.cs`, `WorldNet.More.cs`, `SpellPlans.More.cs`, `ArcanaVfx.cs`. The Tower's 21k cells are generated alike on every peer (`TowerShape`); only base cell and timing are sent. Lasting states live in `Combatant.Status` (`BodyStatus`); the Wheel's and Justice's glows are the body outline (`PlayerOutlines`).
- **XVII–XXI** (Star, Moon, Sun, Judgement, World): `.Tarot3.cs`, `TarotVfx3.cs`. Judgement is written as Arcane Judgment is (`kind` = `TarotCircle`), then slams (`SlamJudgement`, `WheelOp.Slam`: every peer works out the same cells). The Moon fills from `OnDamaged` (`.Justice.cs`). The Star's through-walls outline (`SME/LitMask` + `SME/LitOutline`) and The Sun's inverted body (`SME/Invert`) are extra body materials set by `PlayerOutlines`. The Sun's damage follows what each player's own screen shows (`SunGazeWatch`).
- **The World**: sphere in `Voxels/VoxelWorld.Sphere.cs`: one op changes all its cells, meshes catch up later (`StepLate` / `FinishLate`), hidden by `WorldView`'s ball. It never replaces anything indestructible: `WorldSpot` moves it somewhere it fits. Its shell is its own indestructible palette entries until someone in it dies. Taken bodies: `BodyStatus.Taken` / `Vanished` (`PlayerMotor.Held`). What the two see: `UI/WorldScreen.cs`, `UI/WorldCinematic.cs` (a stage on layer 31 far below the map).
- **Death** (reworked from an aura): `.Death.cs`, `SpellPlans.Death.cs`, `WorldNet.Death.cs`, `DeathVfx.cs`. The charge is Mana Siphon's motor (`Motor.Siphon`) with its own pose (`DeathDash`); it goes through everyone. `BodyStatus.DeathMarked` (for good, until death) and `BileFrom/BileAt` (doomed). The vomit is a breath of kind 2 (`BreathView`); bile ground is its own palette entries (`VoxelWorld.BileValue`, like ice) drying off after `BileCoatTime`; standing on it slows (`PlayerMotor.InBile`) unless the hand holds Death. The old aura's `WorldNet.DeathAura` op and `DeathView` are no longer used.
- **The Hanged Man**: one yank (`HangedLift` in `HangedYank`), no longer a toggle; `PlayerMotor` zeroes the upward speed when the noose lets go.
- **The Star**: the count per cast is in its op (`StarFx.stars.Length`); `StarBonus` on the server.
- **Botany** (Sunflower, Cactus, Fly Trap): `.Botany.cs`, `SpellPlans.Botany.cs`, `WorldNet.Botany.cs`, `BotanyVfx.cs`. One pot per caster (`PotFx`: held / ground / flying), its position worked out alike everywhere (`WorldNet.PotPosition`, held: `SpellPlans.PotPoint`). Models are the user's prefabs in `Resources/VFX` rebuilt by `PotModel` (bottom-middle origins; plants placed over the pot where the user modelled them; the cactus's one spike copied round its body; fly trap jaws are "Cube (2)/(3)", the sunflower's glowing face is its Yellow parts). Holding a pot blocks other cards and the shield (`HoldingPot` in `BusyChanneling`); the Fly Trap's throw is `CancelCard`. Eaten bodies: `BodyStatus.EatenBy/EatenUntil` (hidden by the animator, held at the mouth by the motor / dummy, counted as `Hexed`).
- **Pine Tree**: a Botany pot (`PotPine` = 5; 4 is Smallify Hex's). `SpellServer.Pine.cs`; its size over time is `SpellPlans.PineSize` (looks and the burst both use it). At the burst the pot and tree grow together to 6x (`PinePotScale`; the server body's box follows it). A held pot is set upright on the ground below (`ServerPotDrop`); a dropped or thrown one stays as it lies (frozen if at rest). Thrown, each hard hit on voxels breaks a bite (`PineSmash`, `PotHits`) and it bounces (`PineBounce`); the hit volume (`InPine`) is the plant's model box at 3x narrowing to the top. Pressed again: thrown (`ThrowPot(pc, kind)`); dropped, not ended, when knocked down.
- **Hammerang**: `SpellServer.Rang.cs`, `WorldNet.Rang.cs` (ops 162-164), `RangVfx.cs`, `SpellPlans.Rang.cs`; hooked through the Arms reset/tick/interrupt/clear. Server-simulated, sent 30x a second and drawn 0.07 s behind. Only the 0.3 s windup is cancelled by a knock; once thrown it always comes back (through walls) unless its thrower dies.
- **Compressed Shot, Ephemerate, Cloud Step, Submerge, Bubble Bomb**: `.Gale.cs`, `SpellPlans.Gale.cs`, `WorldNet.Gale.cs`, `GaleVfx.cs` (`GaleBodyFx` draws the per-body states). Owner movement in `PlayerMotor` (`Concentrating`, `Ephemerate`, `CloudStep`). `BodyStatus.EphemFrom/Until`, `SubFrom/Until` count as `Folded` (untouchable) and are hidden by the animator. Submerge (5 mana up front) stops regeneration while under (or diving in) through `ManaState.Sunk` (set in `RefreshFlow`). A Bubble Bomb's burst is water for a moment (`Splashes` in `InWater`). Ephemerate's fling uses `SpellServer.QuietKnock` (no combo-break count; the Fly Trap's limp bite uses it too).
- **Smallify Hex**: fills like Mind Hex (caster near). `BodyStatus.SmallFrom/SmallUntil`, size from `SpellPlans.SmallScale` (players: the animator scales the model; dummies: their transform). Bumps in `TickSmall`.
- **Temperance**: the first passive (`CostType.Passive`, shown as "P"). Max health from passives: `Cards.HealthBonus`; the mana cap is `ManaState.Cap`.

- **The Emperor** scales damage from outside the circle: from where the attacker stands, or `DamageFrom` (set it round a `Hit` that comes from somewhere else: meteors, a zombie's bite).
- **Wheel of Fortune**: `.Wheel.cs`, `WheelVfx.cs`. **Justice**: `.Justice.cs` (`Combatant.Killer`, `BodyStatus.JusticeOn`).

## Map hazards (`Hazards/`, `SpellServer.Hazards.cs`)
- Everything but meteors is a pure function of the round's clock (`MapClock`: `State.fightStart/fightEnd`), so there are no ops. Trains: timetable seeded from round + start time + track index, the same on every peer. `MapClock` takes the arena: a practice chamber's hazards are off (liquids at their starting level) until its player presses P (`Arena.practiceHazards`, `HazardOp.Practice`), then start at once, skipping their waits.
- **Meteors** are fireballs with `meteor` set (as Necromatic Blast's are with `necro`): use `f.Radius`, not `FireballRadius`, for anything that meets a fireball in flight. Their crater (`ServerMeteorCrater`) leaves no debris, on purpose (performance).
- **Out of bounds**: `ArenaLimits` boxes in the arena prefab (sides and top; the bottom is `killHeight`), else the voxels' bounds + 10 m; outside: warned (`GameUI.Bounds.cs`), shattered after 3 s.
- Liquids (`Liquid`): `RisingWater` counts in `InWater`; floating is `PlayerMotor.SwimTick` / `RisingWater.FloatLine`. It washes away summoned ground (`RisingWater.ServerWash`).
- **`SpikeWalls`** (Understation): close along the box's local Z, solid kinematic blocks on Default; touch is spikes included. **`ClosingZone`** (Green Plains): outside the circle shares the water's doubling count (`Drown`), only in its own arena/chamber (`Practice.SlotAt`).

## Collection rules
- **Secret rarity**: never in packs or The Fool's rolls, not counted until owned. The World appears blacked out in the deck once you own every other Tarot card (`Collection.SecretReady`, `UI/SecretReveal.cs`). ALL CARDS in the admin panel leaves it to be found.
- **Companion cards** (`CardInfo.companion`, `Cards.CompanionOf`): never in a pack; given with the cards they go with (Remote Detonation, with anything that places flame bombs), shown behind that card in the shop.

## Engine gotchas
- **Off-screen stages** (layer 31): keep the camera's far plane past the screen outline's fade (`Practice.ViewDistance`) or it paints the empty background grey; `SME/Glow` writes no depth prepass, so outlines of what's behind show through it.
- **Squashing a body** goes through `ProceduralAnimator.Fold` (it resets the model's scale every frame).
- **Strength** grows the model after the pose is solved: props placed by formula go through `SpellPlans.GrowPoint` / `BodyGrow`.
- **Ink hull** (`SME/Outline`) pushes out from the mesh's origin: centre the mesh on it, one piece per limb. Skinned meshes: `SME/HullOutline`.
- **Weapon prefabs** are ProBuilder: instantiate to build the meshes, copy them out (`SwordModel.Part`), destroy.
